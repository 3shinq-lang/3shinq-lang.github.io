Sovereign Assistant

A personal, locally-run AI assistant.

What this app does

Sovereign Assistant is a personal, self-hosted AI assistant that runs entirely on the owner's own computer. As one of its features it connects to the user's own Google Calendar account, with the user's explicit consent, in order to read, display and manage that user's calendar events. It is not a public or multi-tenant service, and it does not collect data from anyone other than its owner.

Google Calendar integration

The integration requests only the Google Calendar scopes it needs (calendar.readonly and calendar.events) and acts solely on the calendar of the account that granted access. The OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's machine and are never sent anywhere except to Google's own APIs.
